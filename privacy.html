Privacy policy

Capybara Talks. Effective 19 October 2026.

Capybara Talks ("the app") is a personal desktop app for macOS and Windows for making narrated videos. This policy explains what the app receives from Google when you connect your Google Drive, how it uses and stores it, how long it keeps it, and how to remove its access. Connecting Google Drive is optional; the app works without it.

What the app accesses

  • Only the Drive files the app works with. The app asks for Google's drive.file permission, which Google shows as "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app". That covers the files the app creates, and files you choose to open with it; the app offers no way to open other files, so in practice it reaches only its own backups. It cannot see, list, open, change or delete any other file or folder in your Drive, such as your photos or documents. Google enforces this limit.
  • The email address of the Google account you connect. The app reads it from Drive's own account information (it asks for no separate email permission) and keeps only the address, not your name or photo, so it can note which account holds each backup.

The app does not ask for your Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Google Photos or any other Google data.

How the app uses it

  • It uploads backup copies of your project's videos and audio to your Drive only when you choose to back up, after showing how many files and how much data will be uploaded.
  • When one of those files is missing on one of your computers, it can download the backup from your Drive, and uses it only after checking that it matches the original exactly.
  • For each backup, it records the Drive file ID, a link to the file, the account's email address, the upload time, the size and a checksum in your project, so your other computers can find the backup. The link opens only for your account: the app never shares a backup with anyone or makes it public.

Where it is stored

  • Your Google sign-in is saved only in your operating system's secure credential store (the Keychain on a Mac, Credential Manager on Windows), on each computer where you connect. It is never written to the app's files or logs.
  • Files travel directly between your computer and Google's servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The app has no server of its own.
  • The backup records are saved in your projects on your computers. The app also copies them, with the rest of each project, to the private GitHub repository it uses to sync your projects between your computers.
  • Files downloaded from your Drive are kept in the app's media cache, inside the app's data folder on that computer.
  • The app's logs on your computer may name backup files and their Drive file IDs.

Sharing

The app does not send data it receives from Google to its developer. It shares it only as described above: the backup records sync with your projects to your private GitHub repository. It does not sell the data or use it for advertising or analytics.

Files restored from your Drive are your project's videos and audio. No feature of the app sends them to an outside service; the app's AI features work on script text, prompts and pictures from your own library, not on your backups. If a future version adds such a feature, this policy is updated first and the app asks for your agreement before it sends a file.

Capybara Talks does not use data received from Google Workspace APIs, including Google Drive, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models, and does not transfer it to anyone for that purpose.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

The app has no server, so its developer holds none of this data. It is kept only on your computers, in your Google Drive and in your private GitHub repository, and you can delete it there.

  • Your Google sign-in stays in your computer's credential store until you disconnect Google Drive in the app, which deletes it from that computer. If you have removed the app's access in your Google Account, the app deletes the saved sign-in the next time it tries to use it. Each computer keeps its own sign-in.
  • Remove the app's access at any time: open the third-party apps and services page of your Google Account, choose Capybara Talks and remove its access (Google's steps). After that, the app can no longer reach your Drive from any of your computers.
  • Backups in your Drive stay until you delete them in Google Drive, like any other file. Removing the app's access does not delete them.
  • Files downloaded from your Drive stay in the app's media cache on that computer until you delete them.
  • Backup records, including the account's email address, stay in your project until you delete the project. Because projects sync through a Git repository, earlier versions stay in that repository's history until you remove them there.

For questions about your data or deleting it, write to the contact address below.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Changes

Before the app uses data from Google in a new way, this policy is updated and the app asks for your agreement. Other changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
