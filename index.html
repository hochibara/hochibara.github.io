Capybara Talks

A personal desktop app for making narrated videos.

Capybara Talks is a desktop app for macOS and Windows, made by its developer for their own video work. It is not offered for download. It helps take a video from script to cut: reviewing the script, arranging clips into groups on a timeline, writing narration scripts, and generating narration and background music with AI voice and music services. Subtitle editing and video export are being added.

How it uses Google Drive

Google Drive is optional. After you connect your Google account and choose to back up, the app uploads backup copies of your project's videos and audio to your own Google Drive. On another of your computers, it can download those backups when a file is missing there.

Google shows the permission the app asks for as "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (the drive.file permission). In practice, these are the backup files the app created itself. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive, such as your photos or documents.
